# Bastille Networks

> Bastille Networks is a wireless airspace cybersecurity company that detects, localizes, and monitors radio-frequency emitting devices and threats across 50+ wireless protocols (Bluetooth, BLE, Wi-Fi, cellular, IoT, and more). Its platform gives enterprises, government agencies, and data centers real-time inventory of wireless devices, insider-threat and rogue-device detection, and localization to a 1-3 meter radius, integrating via an API-led architecture with SIEM/SOAR/XDR/CAASM tooling (Splunk, ArcSight, QRadar). Bastille is a portfolio company of Bessemer Venture Partners.

This is an API Evangelist network profile. Bastille exposes integration APIs to enterprise customers (SIEM/SOAR/XDR/ticketing/MDM), but does not publish a public developer portal, OpenAPI specification, or self-serve API documentation.
